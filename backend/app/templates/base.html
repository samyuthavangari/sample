Healthy Heart Habits ❤️

Maintaining a healthy heart is essential for long life. Here are some scientifically proven lifestyle choices that lower your risk of heart disease:

🥦 Eat Nutritious Food

Include more vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and lean proteins. Reduce fried foods, added sugar, and processed items.

🏃 Stay Active

At least 30 minutes of moderate exercise 5 days a week improves circulation, lowers cholesterol, and keeps the heart strong.

🚭 Avoid Smoking & Alcohol

Smoking damages arteries and increases heart attack risk. Excess alcohol raises blood pressure and weakens heart muscles.

🧘 Manage Stress

Chronic stress impacts your blood pressure. Practice yoga, meditation, deep breathing, or simple relaxation daily.

💤 Sleep Well

7–8 hours of quality sleep per night helps your body repair, balances hormones, and supports heart health.

🩺 Regular Checkups

Get routine checkups for blood pressure, cholesterol, and glucose. Early detection prevents complications.
